## FAQ

**How do I transfer domains between registrars without downtime?**

Unlock your domain at the current registrar, obtain the authorization code from your account dashboard, and initiate transfer at your new registrar by entering that code. Keep DNS nameservers unchanged during transfer so your website and email continue functioning. The transfer typically completes within 5-7 days, and most registrars add one year to your registration period automatically.

**What's the difference between domain privacy and anonymity services?**

Domain privacy (WHOIS privacy) replaces your contact information in public registries with proxy details while you remain the legal owner. Anonymity services like Njalla register domains in their name and grant you control through agreements, hiding ownership legally. Most users need only standard privacy; anonymity serves specific high-privacy situations with unique legal considerations.

**Should I register domains where I'm hosting or use separate registrars?**

Separating domain registration from hosting provides flexibility when changing hosts and reduces single-point-of-failure risks. [Spaceship](https://www.spaceship.com) offers advanced domain management tools letting you maintain control independently of hosting decisions. However, integrated services from providers offering both can simplify management for users prioritizing convenience over separation of concerns.
